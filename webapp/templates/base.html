NeuroScan

A MobileNetV2 brain MRI classifier built with TensorFlow, Keras, Flask and Bootstrap. For research and education only, not a medical device.

Dataset: Jamil and Khan (2025), Mendeley Data, doi:10.17632/82mtzd8x72.2. Sample scans come from the dataset's test split.
